### 2026-07-04 — The 42-minute test file (v0.177.0) — SETTLED
**Symptom:** full gate 47 min locally, CI wall 42+ min, no matter the hardware. **Root cause:** vitest runs a file's tests *sequentially*; `mood.test.ts` alone ran 42.2 CI minutes (three log-only "instrument" tests burned 70 season-sims ending in `expect(true)`), and retirement/advance each walked five separate 5–10-season leagues to assert independent end-state invariants. **Fix:** instruments behind `it.skip` (house convention), file splits, and *shared trajectory tests* asserting every invariant every season — stronger and less than half the sims. Timing-audit alarm added (+25% over baseline warns). **Evidence:** CHANGELOG `[0.177.0]`; `scripts/test-timing-audit.mjs`. **Lesson:** a diagnostic that asserts nothing does not belong in the gate; per-season assertions beat end-state point checks.

### 2026-07-04 — Carousel test zero-firings (v0.177.0) — SETTLED
**Symptom:** front-office test: 0 HC firings over its run; looked like v0.176 killed the coaching carousel. **Root cause:** NOT a regression — league firing rate probed unchanged vs a v0.174.1 worktree (18 vs 21 firings over 4 seeds); the test ran 2 cycles under a "3yr" name with a single-seed ≥1 floor, and the v0.176 economy reshuffled that seed's trajectory to a quiet landing (top seat pressure 45 vs threshold ~50). **Fix:** test runs its titular 3 cycles. **Evidence:** CHANGELOG `[0.177.0] Fixed`; probe pattern in `packages/truth-arbiter/data/_carousel_probe.mjs`. **Lesson:** single-seed loose floors on pressure-ramping systems are one bounce from false alarms; discriminate seed-reshuffle vs regression with the worktree A/B before touching engine code.

### 2026-07-03 — Cap-usage sag under growth (v0.176.0) — SETTLED
**Symptom:** with the cap growing 6%/yr, in-season usage slid 85→81% despite the CBA cash floor. **Two root causes:** (1) the cash floor's *trailing-only* 4-season window priced 89% of older, smaller caps (≈81% of the current cap) — growth quietly unwound the floor; (2) escalating deal shapes made the same dollar anchors realize ~12% less current-year spend (Y1 < APY; back-loaded years die with early releases). **Fixes:** window includes the season in progress (real CBA counts the season underway); veteran anchors lifted +10% in *lockstep* with the auction divisor ("headline APY above realized cap cost" is the real market's signature). **Evidence:** CHANGELOG `[0.176.0] Changed`; `teamCashFloorStatus` in `packages/engine/src/contracts/cash.ts`. **Lesson:** %-of-cap invariants silently decay under a growing denominator; anchors and their divisors must move together or multipliers double-count.

### 2026-07-02..03 — The dead v0.154 QB-churn damper (v0.175.0) — SETTLED
**Symptom:** Goatinator #1-overall QB share regressed 76→67% (real bar 75). **Root cause (two-layer):** the v0.154 record-aware re-sign damper had NEVER fired in any sim — `applyResigningWindow` compared `lastRecord.seasonNumber === league.seasonNumber`, but finalize rolls `seasonNumber` to the upcoming season while history entries are dated to the just-played one; N === N+1 never matches. Unit tests passed because they injected wins directly. Meanwhile v0.170/173 added retention channels with no record-awareness at all; a probe decomposed non-QB #1 picks *exactly* 4/4/4 across re-sign window, extensions, and cash-lag FA overpay (the worst teams had become the strongest vet-QB bidders). **Fix:** `lastSeasonWins()` canonical accessor (compares `seasonNumber - 1`) + record-aware carve-outs in all three channels (≤6-win teams never extend or lag-boost-bid non-STAR QBs). Goatinator restored to 75/75 exact. **Evidence:** CHANGELOG `[0.175.0]`; doc comment on `lastSeasonWins` in `transactions/re-sign.ts`. **Lessons:** offseason code reading `seasonHistory` must date against `seasonNumber - 1`; integration-shaped regression tests catch what unit injection hides; decompose by CHANNEL before tuning anything.

### 2026-07-02 — Birth-cap compliance: the reverted approach (v0.174.0 → v0.174.1) — SETTLED, approach FENCED
**Symptom:** leagues born over the hard cap (4/32 teams on the default seed). **v0.174.0 (fenced):** simulate day-one compliance (restructures + cuts at birth) — passed targeted gates, failed the FULL gate: 20 tests/8 files (sub-53 rosters, contract-less FA-pool players, holes in depth charts, non-empty day-one transaction log); adding a refill scramble made it worse (market gates strand teams short; the offseason machine *prunes* unsigned casualties from `league.players`). **v0.174.1 (kept):** a born-over book is a GENERATOR PRICING ERROR, not history — trim current-year bases at generation. Traps encoded: seed deals are MID-STREAM (`yearOfDeal = realYears − yearsRemaining`; index 0 is wrong) and leading-year bases are 100% guaranteed, so a guarantee-respecting floor strands teams over — floor at vet-min only. **Evidence:** CHANGELOG `[0.174.1]`, `[0.174.0]`; trim code in `league/generate.ts`. **Lesson:** don't simulate history to fix a generator bug; targeted gates ship slices, only the full gate guards pushes.

### 2026-07-02 — Restructures shipped dormant — ON PURPOSE (v0.172.0) — SETTLED precedent
**Symptom:** the new restructure pass fired zero times across whole sims. **Root cause:** honest — no team was ever cap-pinned (equilibrium March-point max ~81% vs the 92% win-now trigger; short flat deals shed ~15pp per rollover). **Decision:** do NOT lower the trigger ("fake behavior"); the contract-shape slice (v0.176) later created honest, thin pinning. **Evidence:** CHANGELOG `[0.172.0]`; comments in `transactions/restructures.ts`. **Lesson → unwritten law #3:** never weaken a gate to make dormant behavior fire.

### 2026-06-30..07-01 — The PFF talent-score marathon (v0.168.0) — SETTLED
**Symptom:** mature leagues drifted to QB 25% / K+P 18% of the ELITE/STAR pool while OL/LB collapsed (S0 was realistic; the equilibrium wasn't). **Root cause:** per-season re-grade from current key-skill average vs ONE absolute cross-position threshold; QB/K key skills are all technical/mental (decline late) so they ratchet into the star pool. **Fix:** sticky `Player.talentScore` (EWMA α=0.35 of within-position percentile) → fixed cuts; counts float, one bad year doesn't demote, washed vets slide out over ~2–3y. **Two regressions found en route:** (a) FA washout read the *relative* grade → weak FAs dodged washout → `league.players` grew unbounded → forward sims crawled (fix: washout uses the absolute grade); (b) cap usage fell below floor — the old cap level was PROPPED by QB/K star inflation since contracts price off tier (the cap/star coupling; interim cut-nudge, honestly retired in v0.171 and decoupled properly by v0.173's cash floor). **Evidence:** CHANGELOG `[0.168.0]`, `[0.171.0]`; `season/talent-score.ts`. **Lessons:** absolute cross-position thresholds + asymmetric aging = pile-up; calibration constants can be load-bearing for unrelated systems.

### 2026-06-23 — Over-seasons scoring drift: the static fix PROVEN INFEASIBLE (v0.165.0) — SETTLED, static path FENCED
**Symptom:** points/game 24.2→27.5 over 10 seasons. **Root cause:** aging curves (real, per the Actuary) decline short-window positions early → mature-league coverage erodes while QB survivorship lifts qbPlay → QB-weighted passEdge climbs. **Fenced:** the full static reconcile (aging-consistent generation + re-baseline) was *implemented and proven infeasible* — the developed equilibrium moves and QB survivorship is irreducible; drift is season-varying, no static offset nulls it. **Fix:** dynamic per-season league-mean re-centering (`leagueRecenter` in `games/drive-sim.ts`) — a uniform shift preserving margins/records/draft order; a stationarity gate (`pointsDrift`) went into the Scorekeeper. **Evidence:** CHANGELOG `[0.165.0]`. **Lesson fence:** drive-sim constants are the WRONG lever for scoring drift; verdict from the same arc: the foundation was sound ("NOT rotten — one gen↔dev seam + a test blind spot").

### 2026-06-12..25 — The #1-overall QB share campaign (v0.145→v0.166) — SETTLED (residual at #2)
**Arc:** GMSim's #1 pick was 23% QB vs real 75. Fixes layered across releases: QB-room-gated slot premium (v0.145), the re-sign window itself (v0.148 — pre-fix ~45% of primary passers changed teams in ONE offseason vs real 78.4% stay rate, mass-producing QB desperation *everywhere* instead of at the top), record-aware churn design (v0.154), record↔QB coupling `passEdge qbPlay 0.5→0.65` (v0.164), and THE ROSEN RULE (v0.166): a franchise-dev rookie "settles" the QB room — except at premier picks, where holding the pick is itself evidence the plan failed; the abandon floor decays across picks #1→#3 to match the real 75/44/25 curve (a flat window over-drafted #3 to 49%). **Evidence:** CHANGELOG `[0.148.0]`, `[0.154.0]`, `[0.164.0]`, `[0.166.0]`; `draft/team-needs.ts` qbUpgradeDesire comments. **Status:** #1 exact at 75/75 (v0.176 Goatinator); **OPEN RESIDUAL:** #2-overall QB share 39 vs real 44.

### 2026-06-17 — EDGE top-10 flood (v0.163.0) — SETTLED
**Symptom:** EDGE 25%+ of top-10 picks vs real 14%; probe showed only 21% of top-10 edges were their team's board #1 — positional value was vaulting board-rank-4 edges over board-top prospects. **Fix:** `POSITION_DRAFT_VALUE` re-derived from veteran-APY→draft-capital blend (spread compressed), grade-dependent rookie realization so blue-chips separate on the board. **Evidence:** CHANGELOG `[0.163.0]`; `draft/position-value.ts` header comments. **Lesson:** need-side value multipliers must not overpower the talent signal; measure board-rank-at-pick, not just the mix.

### 2026-06-03 — Media spread-gradient arc (v0.113→v0.116) — SETTLED
**Symptom:** real media disagreement EXPLODES down-board (spread-by-tier 6.6×); GMSim outlets read alike (1.1×). **Path:** per-outlet grade-lean scaled by uncertainty (v0.113, → 3.1×) hit a structural bottleneck — the shallow talent pyramid let a bigger lean leap contested mids OVER blue-chips. ### 2026-05 era — Mood saturation (v0.17–v0.18.x) — SETTLED, gated forever
**Symptom:** after a few seasons the whole league sat pegged near mood 100. **Root cause:** every mood driver leaned slightly positive; offseason drift only partially pulled back. **Fix:** all drivers balanced zero-mean across the league; the long-horizon equilibrium gate (now `season/mood-equilibrium.test.ts`: 4 seeds × 8 seasons, league mood mean within ±5 of setPoint mean, <2% saturation) guards it permanently. **Evidence:** the test's own comments; CHANGELOG `[0.18.x]`. **Lesson:** slow one-directional biases need long-horizon equilibrium gates, not single-season checks.
